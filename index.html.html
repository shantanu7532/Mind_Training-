Key Concepts to Know

Occult Science (Adhyatma)

The hidden science that we have to discover within ourselves. The man who respects nature and occult science will be helped by it.

In-Service vs Service

Service: money is involved. In-service: money is not involved. In-service means ego is zero — doing activity for the benefit of everyone.

Value Education vs General Education

Value education = Knowledge (deep understanding). General education = Information (surface level facts).

Self Love = Guru Love

Only self love can happen. Self love means Guru love — true love starts within and connects to the teacher.

Gyanendriya — Organs of Knowledge

The 5 sense organs that collect information (input only):

  • Ears (Shravana) → Hearing
  • Eyes (Chaksha) → Seeing
  • Nose (Ghrana) → Smelling
  • Tongue (Rasana) → Tasting
  • Skin (Tvak/Sparsha) → Touching

Karmendriya — Organs of Action

The 5 action organs that perform actions (output):

  • Mouth (Vak) → Speaking
  • Hands (Pani) → Holding
  • Feet (Pada) → Movement
  • Anus (Payu) → Excretion
  • Reproductive Organs (Upastha) → Reproduction

Conscious Meditation (Jagrat Dhyan)

Every day before sleeping, review your whole day — see the picture without React and Response. This is called Conscious Meditation.

Transformation of Knowledge = Dharma

Giving knowledge is the most best thing in the universe. Transformation of knowledge is called Dharma.

Karma Siddhant

The law of karma — no forgiveness (Madhe Konala Mafi Nahi). Your questions are you. Your answers are you.

Human Can Only Be Taught By Human

When the student is ready, the teacher appears. Learning requires a human teacher — not just books or information.

Important Aspects of Life

Three things to keep in balance:

  • Mental
  • Physical
  • Financial
